South HPGe working comparison baseline — 2026-10-03

Decision
Use the recovered South small-vial 25 cm export as the first working efficiency
curve. Its native ANH file is not needed to interpolate the exported table.
Use QG-conditioned line response for reanalysis of the SAME physical count, and
the pooled QG response knots as a fallback shape if the export is unavailable.
This implements the user's assumption that QuantumGold is mostly correct.

What is saved and how to reproduce
Run python build_baseline.py from this folder; no installation is needed.
All inputs are copied byte-for-byte, with original locations and SHA256 in
provenance.json. The 31 original QG report hashes were checked on preparation.
The pre-existing chronology receipt retains all source evidence and flags.
The outputs are study data within FluxForge; no production calibration gate or
reactor model was changed. Existing issue-197 calibration/source recovery and
QG-yield QC provide the context; this package adds an explicit working scenario.

Recovered curve
South Small Vial 25cm.csv was attached by Robert on April 30, 2025. Preserve all
raw values, including negative low-energy rows. Working units are percent:
epsilon_fraction = exported_value/100. This assumption is supported by the
nominal long-lived QG comparisons, but source units were not declared. Use only
positive adjacent table brackets; no extrapolation or bridging invalid rows.
The numeric Error field is preserved, not interpreted as a standard uncertainty.
Do not multiply the recovered table by the median QG/table ratio: it is a
consistency diagnostic, not an independently established correction factor.

QG-conditioned reconstruction
For each positive reported line:
epsilon_effective = N_QG / (A_line_uCi * 37000 * live_seconds * (RAD_INT/100)).
This is a line-specific effective response, which can include historical
attenuation, dead-time/processing, decay and activity-reference conventions.
It is not an independently identified intrinsic detector efficiency.
For a refitted line in the SAME spectrum, at the SAME reference convention:
A_new_Bq = N_new * (A_QG_uCi * 37000 / N_QG).
build_baseline.refit_qg_activity(row, N_new) supplies this working conversion.
This lets FluxForge improve net-area extraction while keeping QG's response
assumptions. It does not recreate QG's multi-line isotope-combination algorithm.
Keep signed new net areas and their fit uncertainties; never turn an upper limit
or failed fit into a positive detection. With an exact working response, the
conditional counting-only standard error is abs(A_QG_Bq/N_QG)*sigma_N_new;
shared response uncertainty remains unspecified, not zero or independently
inferred from correlated QG counts/activity. Report that condition in results.

Fallback curve when the recovered table is unavailable
qg_conditioned_response_knots.csv pools South 25 cm lines only when coefficients
match the recovered profile at printed precision, RAD_INT>1 and the simple
count-window average-decay factor exceeds 0.99. At each exact energy use median
effective response. Minimum/maximum are descriptive spread, not uncertainty.
Interpolate linearly within the knot energy range only. This curve is a working
comparison of similar 25 cm geometries, not a geometry-transfer calibration.
The raw effective points and every exclusion are retained in the line lookup.
No forced polynomial smoothing, invented covariance or uncertainty floor.
The median response/export ratio is 1.019 across the 156 pooled rows, but the
100.1 keV ratio reaches about 1.28 and the 113.7 keV ratio about 1.20. Preserve
this energy dependence: a single global rescaling would hide it. The empirical
curve may embed historical attenuation/processing differences, especially at
low energy. Prefer a same-count anchor when comparing refitted low-energy lines.

Known exceptions
Sc48 983.5 and 1312.1 keV printed RAD_INT=1.00 is the established ~100x display/
yield issue. These points are excluded from accepted anchors and pooled fits.
All RAD_INT<=1 points remain ambiguous and are likewise excluded. Values are
preserved; independent yield substitutions belong to the separate Sc48 study.
sc48_corrected_yield_scenario.csv reproduces that study's central-yield working
alternative using the recovered curve, ENSDF probabilities and QG net counts.
It provides count-average and simple-decay count-start activities separately;
no QG value is overwritten and no independent yield covariance is invented.
Fe-Cd's near-contact geometry remains separate: no 25 cm response substitution,
inverse-square transfer or pooling. Its otherwise accepted same-count QG line
anchor can be used to compare a refit of that SAME count.
RAFM-A-2hr has no QG report; retain its raw identity and use the recovered curve
with explicitly assumed nominal South 25 cm geometry for a new reduction.
The supplied package does not assign it a fictitious QG response or activity.

Identity and timing
measurement_identity_and_timing.csv exports the existing 32-count hash-bound
joins, live/real durations, QG/ANS working clocks and maintained irradiation
schedule candidates. All 31 available QG acquisition times/durations match the
empirical ANS fields; 30 ASC arrays match ANS arrays. Use QG/ANS clocks as the
working measurement clock. Do not silently shift ASC timestamps by 1 or 3 h.
irradiation_timing_scenarios.csv records elapsed cooling time for each available
EOI candidate under a shared local naive-clock assumption. For RAFM4 the
advisor-correspondence Aug 5 10:28–12:28 E8 interval is the preferred working
scenario because it explicitly names Hirst-RAFM4; retain Aug 4 13:00–15:00 as
the maintained-schedule alternative. Do not apply the RAFM4 correspondence
automatically to all monitors. Original labels bind early counts to RAFM3 and
15d counts to RAFM4; repeated A/B/C/N labels do not establish one specimen.
Count-reference activity comparison can proceed without resolving EOI. For EOI
activity use the selected time scenario and explicit nuclide half-life; test
simple-decay/feeding assumptions separately. Do not infer reactor power from
these dates or cumulative logs.

Finite count duration
If a new reduction assumes simple decay and uniform live fraction, the
count-start response integral is S=(L/R)*[-expm1(-lambda*R)]/lambda;
A_start=N/(epsilon*p*S), while A_count_average=N/(epsilon*p*L).
Do not additionally apply that correction to a QG-reference line anchor unless
the QG activity-time convention is deliberately changed. The historical output
retains both effective and hypothetical count-start response as separate fields.

Attenuation and what detector information can help
For same-count QG response anchoring, preserve the empirical combined response;
do not add another attenuation or coincidence correction over it. For a fresh
recovered-curve activity estimate, unresolved sample attenuation can be set to
unity as an explicit working scenario, not asserted to be physically absent.
A later attenuation calculation needs emitting material/composition, density,
thickness/path distribution and container geometry. Compare energy dependence
and matched lines first, especially low-energy lines and Cu inside V capillary.
Do not impose arbitrary percentage errors on all peaks for missing information.

The existing workbook identifies South serial 31-TP20602A: crystal diameter
6.0 cm, length 6.45 cm, end-cap thickness 0.1 cm, crystal gap 0.4 cm, outer
contact layer 0.07 cm. Workbook explanations distinguish manual, measured,
borrowed and guessed values; preserve them. QG headers identify HpGe(P),
coaxial, 6.0 cm diameter. The workbook supports detector-specific modeling;
it does not establish the 2025 active calibration or settle CSV DI semantics.
geomEffUWHPGe.xlsx is geometric solid-angle information, not full-energy peak
efficiency. Generic manufacturer relative efficiency likewise is not a usable
absolute energy curve. Best later transfer: use the actual detector geometry,
validate photon transport against the recovered 25 cm response, then compute
target/reference geometry ratios. NIST demonstrates measured-vs-computed
efficiency comparisons for complex geometries. No new simulation is run here.

Readiness and comparison meaning
Ready for conditional line/activity comparisons and improved peak fitting using
the declared QG working reference. Such agreement is not an independent test of
absolute efficiency. It can test FluxForge extraction and expose suspect QG
lines; it cannot by itself validate absolute neutron flux or transport C/E.
This distinction does not block the user-authorized empirical comparison.

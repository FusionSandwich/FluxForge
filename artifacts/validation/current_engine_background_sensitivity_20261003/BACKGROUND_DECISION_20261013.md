# RAFM South-background decision and next count test — 2026-10-13

This note extends the same source-bound 30-ASC North/South/ambient-off replay. The reproducible [background leverage receipt](background_decision_analysis.json) is derived by [this read-only script](background_decision_analysis.py) from the pinned three-scenario comparison and forensics. It does not fit a new background scale or select a physical activity.

## What the new check separates

- **Most QG activity disagreement is insensitive to the background choice.** Across the same 81 matched positive activity rows, the median absolute South-versus-North activity shift is **0.118%**. **66/81** shift by less than 5%; among those 66, the median absolute South-versus-QG activity difference is **49.55%**, and 49 exceed 20%. Nine rows shift by more than 10%, six by more than 20%. These are descriptive ratios from a conditional engine replay. They cannot establish that either measured background applies physically or that a QG report is a calibration truth.
- **Weak Co-Cd is a high-leverage exception.** South is 37.74 counts/s, North 12.54 counts/s and raw Co-Cd 41.56 counts/s. Exposure-scaled South subtraction leaves only 9.2% of Co-Cd's whole-spectrum counts. Co-Cd's activity falls from 156.23 Bq with North to 134.33 Bq with South; the ambient-off protocol control gives 246.19 Bq versus QG's 234.95 Bq. The [count forensics](FOLLOWUP_FORENSICS.md) find the QG Co-60 line counts much closer to ambient-off. Saved ANS flags support ambient-off in saved spectra, while final report settings remain unverified. Therefore QG closeness cannot choose a physical background.
- **The present peak estimator adds a separate instability.** A fixed ±5 keV core with fixed sidebands makes the Co-Cd South net counts *larger* than North for both Co-60 lines (7477 versus 6858; 6996 versus 6016), whereas the full campaign's moving IEC pathway makes South *smaller* (5370 versus 6989; 6473 versus 6974). Those fixed-window counts are controls, not qualified peak areas. The reversal shows that background choice and ROI/continuum selection interact. Compare the same fixed ROI, continuum and covariance before attributing a line change to detector background.
- **A more physical native-count model is not yet adequate.** The [South Co-Cd pilot, PR #250](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/pull/250) uses original integer sample and South counts on their own energy grids. All 24 predeclared fixed-response fits had strong lack of fit; two free-normalization controls were unidentifiable. The pilot's signed residuals have excess below and deficits above nominal sample centroids under South and ambient-off. A step continuum and half-bin response challenges reduced deviance but did not pass adequacy. The earlier [North joint-count pilot, PR #244](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/pull/244) also reports lack of fit with its later cross-detector background. None qualifies a physical activity or establishes that a background-scale change resolves Co-Cd.
- **Do not substitute the converted ASC header calibration as a quick fix.** Original Co-Cd ANS energy coefficients are `[-1.69438636, 0.49962062, 6.70974e-08]`, close to the workflow profile `[-1.694, 0.4996, 6.71e-08]`; the converted ASC header instead contains `[0.541, 0.498, 2.605e-07]`. The original ANS is the closer independent record of the acquisition calibration. The pilot's response residuals justify checking calibrated peak shape, summing/pileup and continuum with source data, not choosing a shifted header because it improves a fit.
- **Activity conversion is another independent mismatch.** For count-matched V-52 and Sc-48 lines with QG `RAD INT 1.00`, QG activity per physical line count is roughly 97× and 89× FluxForge, while a Sc-48 `97.60` line is 0.87×. The [Quantum 4.04 manual](https://ludlums.com/images/product_manuals/QTMmanual.pdf) defines intensity per 100 decays, but the installed QG version and exact GammaLib remain unknown. Background substitution cannot correct a line-specific intensity/efficiency/timing convention.

## Decision and next experiment

**Keep South as the same-detector measured-background candidate for every original South sample**, with each sample's live-time scaling and propagated background uncertainty. Its 2025-10-03 acquisition postdates the July–August samples; no earlier South stability record has been bound. Treat that temporal applicability as conditional. **Keep North only as a cross-detector sensitivity control.** North was recorded on 2026-03-02 and identified as a different detector, so apparent QG closeness does not make it the right physical subtraction. Keep the no-ambient run as a separate QG protocol control.

The next useful count experiment is **one predeclared Co-Cd two-line transfer check**. The stronger bare Co-RAFM-1 original ANS (SHA-256 `1eadf548bd66b3da425d8bd891d8523e0ef1f59a9ea4471684f96dc0855675c9`) identifies South, 25 cm and the same original energy polynomial; its converted ASC says 2025-08-27, one day before Co-Cd. Learn only detector peak-shape/centroid behavior from that independent high-count control and document source/cover differences. Existing Co-Cd residuals motivate this design; freeze the transfer model, windows and scoring before applying it to Co-Cd. Use original Co-Cd and native South integer observations, locked windows, and a sample continuum constrained by sidebands rather than QG activity. Require predictive count adequacy for *both* Co-60 lines and a held-out sideband, with calibration/efficiency limitations explicit. A failed transfer means conditional count intervals and a model-failure flag, not a forced background scale or physical Co-Cd activity. PR #250 already supplies the failed fixed-response baseline, so the transfer targets its residual structure rather than repeating its 24 candidates.

In parallel, bind an earlier South background or detector stability log if it exists, and verify the final QG processing state/GammaLib and detector efficiency provenance under existing issues [#229](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/229), [#236](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/236) and [#237](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/237). Use count parity first, then line-specific conversion, then isotope combination, then activation/flux. No new issue is needed for the already tracked blockers. No physical neutron-flux inversion is qualified by these checks.

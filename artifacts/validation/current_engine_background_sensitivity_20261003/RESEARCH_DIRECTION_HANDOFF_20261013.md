## Reconciliation with current FluxForge source and RAFM evidence

1. **GRAVEL row units:** The `c297542` review/root check found row-unit dependence. In draft integration `701288a`, `src/fluxforge/solvers/iterative.py` now uses `(y_i/σ_i)^2` GRAVEL row factors and `y_i/σ_i²` effective MLEM weights; `tests/test_iterative_row_scaling.py` includes an individual-row rescaling counterexample and a GRAVEL reference-weight check. This is an implemented candidate repair under [#194](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/194), not a reason to repeat it in PR #230. These tests were read, not rerun in this milestone; integration/merge status and scientific suitability remain separate.
2. **Uncertainty and interpretation:** Draft integration no longer publishes the ridge pseudo-inverse fallback as GRAVEL/MLEM uncertainty. Its default is unavailable (`NaN` internally) or an explicit re-solve Monte Carlo diagnostic with a stated covariance basis. It labels chi-squared per measurement as descriptive. This narrows the earlier finding, but does not propagate every shared efficiency/nuclear-data covariance or make five response families identify fourteen prior-shaped coefficients. Existing [#195](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/195) and related uncertainty work remain qualification gates; preserve unavailable rather than zero.
3. **Flux representation:** In `701288a`, `set_mcnp_initial_guess(..., flux_units=...)` accepts the argument but interpolates ordinates without an explicit differential/integrated/lethargy conversion. `compare_with_mcnp` divides by an unweighted sum of ordinates. Those operations can support a labeled shape diagnostic only after input representation is established; they do not demonstrate absolute response closure on unequal bins. The current thread does not own this shared workflow edit. The active solver/units owners should check the exact call path before a targeted repair under existing [#200](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/200)/[#195](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/issues/195); do not count this source inspection as a numerical RAFM diagnosis.
4. **South and vendor separation:** The [source-bound forensics](FOLLOWUP_FORENSICS.md) show all 32 original ANS spectra and 31 QG reports identify South; North is cross-detector sensitivity. Co-Cd retains only 9.2% of its whole-spectrum counts after exposure-scaled South subtraction, so temporal background drift matters. Its QG line counts resemble the ambient-off protocol control, while V-52 and Sc-48 `RAD INT 1.00` lines show large line-specific activity-per-physical-count differences. The [Quantum 4.04 manual](https://ludlums.com/images/product_manuals/QTMmanual.pdf) documents intensity per 100 decays, but the installed version/library is unknown. Do not choose a physical background or efficiency scale by QG activity closeness.
5. **Newer model checks:** PR [#250](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/pull/250) reports all 24 fixed-response native South Co-Cd candidates with strong lack of fit and unidentifiable free-normalization controls. PR [#251](https://github.com/FusionSandwich/FluxForge/pull/251) is another opt-in source-bound pilot. Neither admits a physical activity or neutron flux. This handoff does not alter either owner's work or accept their numerical claims without the attached receipts/review.

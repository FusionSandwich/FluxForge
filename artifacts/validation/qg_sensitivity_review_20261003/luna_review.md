## Sensitivity results and scientific limits

The earlier sweep used historical native/audit artifacts whose timestamps predate the in-progress fresh extraction. Treat its numeric results as exploratory only. It evaluated baseline `(4.0 FWHM ROI, 1 sideband channel each, 0 gap)`, wider sidebands `(4.0, 8, 0.5)`, narrower ROI `(2.5, 1, 0)`, and combined `(2.5, 8, 0.5)`, retaining measured background and uncertainty.

The baseline marked the C-300s W-187 zero-net reference row as not detected (~1.65 sigma); all three alternatives raised it above threshold (~3.7–4.6 sigma). The N-300s W-187 row started around 2.03 sigma and rose as high as ~6.2 sigma. Three Sc-48 weak wire matches started below threshold (~1.55–1.99 sigma) and crossed 2 sigma under every alternate configuration. Other weak material associations also became detections under alternate windows. Strong controls Cr-51 (320 keV) and Ta-182 (264 keV) stayed detected across variants, but the 846.764-keV Mn-56 control remained absent in that old all-neighbor sweep. Thus a fixed 2-sigma decision changes materially under plausible ROI/sideband choices; crossing that threshold in a sensitivity variant alone is not detection evidence.

A separate direct fixed-library-energy ROI calculation on five available spectra from the six zero-net rows also showed strong dependence on a one-FWHM sideband gap. For example, C-300s W-187 was ~0.06 sigma with the current local ROI and ~4.67 sigma with the alternative gap; N-300s W-187 shifted from ~2.03 to ~5.65 sigma. These fixed-center local counts are diagnostics, not fitted detections, because they do not account for target selection, multiplet assignment, or a search-wide false-positive rate. One of the six rows lacks a paired raw spectrum.

The ROI calculation measures background-subtracted physical net counts. QG-reported activity/count fields and those physical counts are different quantities; a nonzero raw/local residual does not establish that an isotope is present. Refit choices, centroid mismatch, continuum shape, neighboring lines, and multiple comparisons remain relevant. Any acceptance should preserve the original QG identity/status and report sensitivity results separately.

## Uncertainty and remaining guardrails

Joint multiplet area uncertainty itself is propagated from the full fit-parameter covariance through the area gradient (`PeakFitResult.net_counts_uncertainty`); the targeted path uses that result and applies the ROI uncertainty as a floor rather than double-counting two correlated estimators. The analytical covariance tests passed in the earlier focused run. However, `combine_peak_activities` inverse-variance averages per-line activities as if independent. It does not carry cross-line covariance or shared efficiency/calibration uncertainty, so combined activity uncertainty can be optimistic when those terms matter. The current per-line diagnostic is still useful for checking discordance; do not interpret the weighted uncertainty as a complete common-mode uncertainty budget.

Recommended test guards are: retain ambiguity/energy-mismatch flags through candidate construction and merge; ensure ambiguous alternatives cannot receive activities or match as native IDs; run the sensitivity variants with a fresh input-hash-matched native cache; assert strong controls remain visible; assert the 846.764-keV control emits explicit nearby assignment/failure diagnostics; preserve reference rows and do not tune a setting solely to promote a weak row; and keep uncapped modern-shell GUI review coverage for >12 supported peaks.
